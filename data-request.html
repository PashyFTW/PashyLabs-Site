Data requests (GDPR)

Exercise your privacy rights for Card Champions: Sedma

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. We reply within one month. Making a request is free.

Before you write: the Game stores your progress and settings only on your device, and we do not run accounts or servers. In most cases the quickest way to delete your game data is to clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. To withdraw consent for personalised ads, open Settings → Privacy in the Game. Advertising partners hold their own data; we will forward or help with requests about it.
