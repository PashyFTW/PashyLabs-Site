Classics reborn. The traditional game of Septica, known as Sedma in Czechia, on your phone: quick one-on-one matches against computer opponents, at your own pace, online or offline.

What's in the game

  • One-on-one Septica with French cards, played by the traditional rules
  • Computer opponents at several difficulty levels
  • A tutorial for new players
  • Daily login rewards, levels and a progression map
  • Decks, frames and avatars, most of them unlocked by playing
  • Fully playable offline

More is planned, including Hungarian cards and new game modes.

Fair to play

The game is free to download. It shows optional banner and rewarded ads, and offers optional coin packs and a Premium upgrade that removes ads and adds an exclusive deck and frame. There are no full-screen interstitial ads, and the game is not pay-to-win.
