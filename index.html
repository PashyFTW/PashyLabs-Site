Card Champions: Sedma brings Septica, the trick-taking game played across Romania, Czechia and Central Europe, to Android and iOS. Play one-on-one against computer opponents at several difficulty levels, earn coins, and unlock decks, frames and avatars as you go. It plays fully offline.
