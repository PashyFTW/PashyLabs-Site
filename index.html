Our first game

Card Champions: Sedma brings Septica, the trick-taking game played across Romania, Czechia and Central Europe, to Android and iOS. Play one-on-one against computer opponents at several difficulty levels, earn coins, and unlock decks, frames and avatars as you go. It plays fully offline.

More about the game

About the studio

For our founder, building a game studio was a long-held ambition. Through PashyLabs, we want to bring back the classic card games we played as kids with friends and relatives, on holidays and at family visits, and to recreate some of those magical moments with today's technology.

Sedma is our first title. We hope to bring many more to players and make them popular again: games some of us may have forgotten, and others have yet to discover.
